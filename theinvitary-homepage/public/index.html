Good to know

Before you send the link

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply quickly.

What exactly am I buying?

A personal wedding invitation website on its own link, in the design you choose, with your names, functions, venues and RSVP. You pay once; there is no subscription.

What is the guest list package?

Every guest or family gets a personal link that greets them by name, shows their number of seats and only the functions they're invited to. You add and send them yourself from your couple panel, and see who has opened and replied.

Do my guests need to download anything?

No. The invitation opens in any phone's browser straight from WhatsApp, Instagram or a text message. No app and no account.

How long does it take?

Usually the same day, once we have your details. You can try any design with your names right now, for free.

Can I change details after it's live?

Yes, as often as you like, from your couple panel or by messaging us. Every link updates straight away.

How long does my invitation stay online?

Well past your wedding, so guests can look back at it. Ask us if you need it for longer.

How do I pay?

Message us on WhatsApp after trying your design. We'll confirm the price for your country and the payment options available to you.
